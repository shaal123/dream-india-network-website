About us

A network built on one belief: every person deserves dignity

The Dream India Network is a registered independent charitable trust (Reg. No. 24/1993-94).

Dream India Network emerged from a shared vision to spark meaningful societal change. Fueled by the urgent needs of our communities and the belief in transformative possibilities, we’ve evolved from a grassroots initiative into a powerful force for hope and progress. Our journey, driven by passion, resilience, and a relentless commitment to making a difference, continues to inspire and empower thousands to turn dreams into reality.

Our approach is simple. We listen first to what a community needs. Then we build partnerships and programmes that are practical, lasting and respectful of the person at the centre.

At Dream India Network, our actions are rooted in four core pillars: integrity, compassion, innovation, and collaboration. We believe in creating a world where equality isn’t just a goal – it’s a reality. Guided by this vision, we embrace bold ideas, foster meaningful partnerships, and work tirelessly to meet the unique needs of those we serve. Every step we take builds trust, inspires hope, and creates lasting impact.

Foster Homes

Our Foster Home project is dedicated to giving marginalized children the care, love and opportunities they deserve, offering them a safe space, where they can heal, grow and succeed. With 92 homes across South India, we’re dedicated to helping children from vulnerable backgrounds reach their full potential, with each home providing comfortable living spaces, essential medical care, and enrollment in English-medium schools. So far, more than 700 children have gained safety, stability, and opportunities for comprehensive growth through this initiative. Our projects prioritize safety, education, health, and holistic development, laying the groundwork for lasting transformation.

Poverty, financial challenges, and social exclusion often deny children of the nurturing care they need, forcing single parents or guardians to make the difficult decision of relinquishing custody temporarily. At Dream India Network (DIN), we recognize that institutionalization should always be a last resort. To address this, DIN Foster Homes provide a family-like alternative, offering marginalized children a safe, supportive, and nurturing environment while prioritizing their overall development.

In line with Indian guidelines, each DIN Foster Home accommodates up to 8 children, aged 6–18 years, under the care of trained and compassionate staff. These homes are thoughtfully designed to ensure the children’s safety and holistic growth. Comprehensive support includes, comfortable and high-quality accommodations, access to essential medical services, education at premier English-medium schools to foster a brighter future.

Currently, 92 foster homes across South India, with a strong presence in Bangalore, are supporting 718 children. Since the program’s inception, nearly 1,224 children have benefited from these services. Many of these children have been successfully reintegrated into their families as their situations improved, reflecting DIN’s commitment to reuniting families whenever possible.

Expanding the Vision

Looking to the future, DIN is committed to broadening its impact and reaching thousands more vulnerable children. With the unwavering support of dedicated ambassadors and community partners, DIN aims to establish specialized foster homes tailored to meet the diverse needs of children, including:

  • Homes for children at Risk providing both formal and technical education
  • Homes for special children
  • Children who are differently abled
  • Homes for children infected with HIV/AIDS
  • Homes for those who are intellectually challenged – Special Home
  • Home for children who are Terminally ill

School Adoption

DIN is dedicated to transforming education for underprivileged children through the Adoption of Government and Private Schools program. By collaborating with schools and key stakeholders, we aim to build affordable and inclusive learning environments for disadvantaged communities. Presently, this initiative supports nearly 800 children, with ongoing efforts to adopt more schools and establish premium preschools to broaden our impact.

Quality education is a cornerstone in breaking the cycle of poverty in India. Yet, countless children from impoverished backgrounds face barriers to learning many attend under-resourced government schools, while others are entirely excluded from formal education. At Dream India Network (DIN), we firmly believe that providing quality education is a fundamental responsibility of the government. However, the current challenges demand a collaborative approach where government bodies and non-governmental organizations work together to address educational inequities.

Dream India Network sees immense potential in joining hands with various stakeholders, including schools, communities, and private entities. By leveraging existing infrastructure and human resources, we are developing innovative models designed to make education accessible to even the most disadvantaged children in local communities. Our approach focuses on creating solutions that are: replicable, cost-effective and high-quality. 3 high-quality preschools in South India are providing a strong foundation for early childhood education and several additional initiatives are in the pipeline, aiming to extend our reach to thousands more underserved children.

We explore the following possibilities to provide free education to the needy in various localities through partnerships with the government and private sector:

  1. Adopting government schools and transitioning them into English-medium institutions while ensuring adequate emphasis on the local language.
  2. Adopting existing schools or constructing new private schools.
  3. Establishing high-profile preschools in poverty-stricken and slum areas.
  4. Setting up three additional classrooms in private schools to accommodate special children from the locality, facilitating their education and continued engagement.

Dream India Network’s Gardenia and Hosa Kirana preschools, in collaboration with Building Blocks, Y’s Men, Holy Cross Madiwala, Satyaseva Venkateshpura, and St. Francis School in Nalgonda, are dedicated to nurturing the cognitive, social, and emotional development of underprivileged children.

Education Support

Our commitment to education goes beyond providing access; we actively support marginalized students in realizing their academic goals. Through our Educational Support for the Marginalized initiative, we offer scholarships, mentorship, and guidance to nearly 5,000 students. This program has empowered children from economically and physically disadvantaged backgrounds to pursue careers in fields like medicine, helping them turn their aspirations into reality.

In both urban and rural India, countless students face the daunting challenge of accessing quality education, a reality that calls for immediate and decisive action. Recognizing the urgency of this issue, Dream India Network (DIN), in collaboration with its dedicated partners, is steadfast in its mission to identify and support these students, guiding them towards a brighter educational future.

Dream India Network believes that providing guidance and resources to marginalized students is pivotal in bridging the educational divide. Through various initiatives, we extend crucial support to underprivileged children, touching hundreds of lives each year. Over the past five years alone, DIN has directly impacted nearly 5,000 students by offering scholarships and other forms of assistance. At Dream India Network (DIN), we remain unwavering in our pledge to support and transform the educational journeys of marginalized students. Our mission is rooted in the belief that every child, regardless of their socioeconomic background or location, deserves an equal opportunity to unlock their academic potential and build a brighter future. This includes:

  • Supporting Career-Specific Education: Assisting students pursuing specialized fields such as medicine, empowering them to achieve professional excellence.
  • Aid for Differently-Abled Students: Providing tailored support to students with special needs, ensuring they can access inclusive education and thrive in their chosen paths.
  • Economic Assistance for the Underprivileged: Helping children from economically disadvantaged backgrounds receive a standard education and access the same opportunities as their peers.

Colour the Fallen Stars

‘Colour the Fallen Stars – CFS’ is a district level programme that aims to bring back colour and radiance to the lives of destitute men and women on the streets (warmly called as stars). A team of volunteers & staff with ambulance rescue and rehabilitate them in permanent shelter homes providing homely care and protection.

Our program COLOR THE FALLEN STARS is a cornerstone of our efforts to rescue and rehabilitate destitute individuals. Active in four districts, this program is dedicated to identifying and assisting those who have been forgotten by society. By providing holistic support, including shelter, healthcare, and pathways to reintegration, CFS has already positively impacted the lives of nearly 7,000 individuals. This initiative stands as a testament to our commitment to restoring hope and dignity to those in dire need.

A caring and protective home for every destitute person on the street enabling them to have a happy, relaxed and dignified life in their evening years of life span.

Program Highlights

  • Street rescue operations for destitute individuals aged 18 and above.
  • Rehabilitation services in dedicated facilities such as Grace Home and Grace Hospital.
  • Comprehensive follow-up care to ensure long-term support and integration.
  • Data collection to inform continuous improvement of services.
  • Personalized rapport-building with each rescued individual.

Program process

  • Rescue Operations: Employing an ambulance, a skilled team finds and securely transfers the homeless to an appropriate care facility.
  • Rehabilitation: After arriving at Grace Home or other facilities, individuals receive medical attention, housing, and the necessities for a speedy and dignified rehabilitation.
  • Follow-Up Care: Ongoing assistance guarantees that those who have been rehabilitated maintain their better lives and avoid going back to the streets.
  • Community Integration: When necessary, long-term care is provided or attempts are made to reintegrate people into society.
  • Partnerships: Works in tandem with local authorities, NGOs, and healthcare providers to optimize care and resources.

Grace Hospital

Empowering Lives, Restoring Dignity: A Haven for Destitute Health

Free Grace Hospitals and Nursing Care Centres is a critical aspect of our holistic approach to destitute support. Free Grace Hospitals and Nursing Care Centres are designed to bridge the healthcare gap faced by homeless and destitute individuals. These facilities offer comprehensive medical services, catering to those who have no access to proper healthcare. Alongside our Grace Homes, these hospitals and care centres ensure that every individual receives the medical attention and emotional support they need to recover and thrive. From basic care and shelter to healthcare and long-term rehabilitation, our initiatives work in unison to create a society where no one is left behind.

Dream India Network’s Grace Hospitals and Nursing Caring Centres represent a revolutionary step towards ensuring accessible healthcare for destitute individuals. These facilities are designed to eliminate the barriers that prevent the marginalized from receiving critical medical care, offering not only treatment but also hope and dignity.

Impact at a Glance

  • Each centre is equipped to accommodate 8 to 24 patients, providing round-the-clock care and essential services.

Program Highlights

  • Dedicated healthcare spaces in district/taluk headquarters for destitute patients.
  • Collaborations with private and government hospitals to provide necessary facilities.
  • Comprehensive medical care, including free medication, food, and clothing.
  • Facilities cater to both bedridden patients and ambulatory individuals needing nursing care.

Process of the Program

  • Identification: Destitute and bedridden individuals are identified by the police, public, or DIN staff.
  • Admission: Patients are admitted without the need for an accompanying individual, ensuring no one is turned away.
  • Medical Care: A team of medical professionals provides treatments, health check-ups, and ongoing nursing care.
  • Resource Provision: Essential resources like medicines, meals, and clothing are provided to all patients at no cost.
  • Accessibility: Centres are accessible to individuals in open shelters and permanent homes, ensuring wide reach within the district.
  • Collaborative Operation: Operates in partnership with local government agencies or private entities to enhance the care provided.

These centres not only ensure destitute individuals receive high-quality healthcare but also aim to restore their dignity and improve their quality of life, reflecting Dream India Network’s commitment to serving and uplifting the most vulnerable populations.

Grace Home

Grace Homes for the Destitute provides a safe haven for destitute individuals, offering them care, support, and an opportunity to regain independence. With 13 active homes, these facilities are more than just shelters; they are spaces where individuals can rediscover their self-worth and rebuild their lives. Recognizing the urgent need for such services, we are working to expand our network of Grace Homes to reach even more individuals who need care and support.

Grace Home responds compassionately to the essential issue of destitution, providing a decent and loving environment for the marginalized. Numerous lives have already been positively impacted by this program in a number of areas, and it has plans to reach even more people.

The number of individuals affected

  • Currently, 104 individuals reside in 13 Grace Homes spread across multiple locations, receiving shelter, care, and support.
  • Since its inception, the program has touched the lives of nearly 300 people, offering them a renewed sense of dignity and purpose.

Grace Home is more than just a shelter—it’s a space for transformation and healing. With plans to expand its reach and open additional homes, Dream India Network envisions a future where no one is left without a place to call home. Through its compassionate approach, Grace Home continues to restore hope, rebuild lives, and reaffirm the belief that every individual deserves a chance to thrive in a supportive and caring environment.

Program Highlights

  • Offers a protective residential care setting with a capacity of 8 individuals per home.
  • Provides comprehensive services including health care, nutritious meals, and recreational activities.
  • Focuses on personal development to promote independence and self-fulfillment.
  • Encourages family reintegration when possible.
  • Planning to expand to three homes per premise in multiple locations.

Process of the Program

  • Intake: Willing individuals are brought in through volunteer referrals or direct outreach initiatives.
  • Residential Care: Residents are accommodated in homes managed by dedicated teams ensuring their safety and well-being.
  • Support Services: Ongoing provision of medical care, healthy meals, and life skills development.
  • Reintegration Efforts: Whenever feasible, efforts are made to reunite individuals with their families.
  • Expansion and Adaptation: Addressing challenges such as space limitations creatively to facilitate the expansion of services.
  • Diverse Care: Categorising homes based on specific needs, such as homes for those who are intellectually challenged and temporary shelters for women and men in distress.

Open shelter

Supporting Homes for the Homeless program is a collaborative initiative focused on strengthening the capabilities of organizations managing shelters for destitute and homeless individuals. This program bridges gaps in resources and care, ensuring these shelters provide effective support and rehabilitation.

Impact at a Glance

  • Each center can accommodate 8 to 24 individuals, benefiting hundreds of destitute and homeless people across multiple districts.

Objective

The program aims to enhance the capacity of shelters to cater to the needs of the marginalized population, ensuring they receive comprehensive care and rehabilitation services.

Highlights

  • Provision of healthcare assistance to shelters.
  • Facilitation of networking opportunities for improved care provision and rehabilitation.
  • Impact: Contributing to the overall well-being and societal stability of destitute and homeless individuals across districts.

Process

The Supporting Homes for the Homeless program employs a strategic and collaborative approach to ensure shelters offer high-quality care and comprehensive support to destitute individuals.

  1. Partnerships with Governmental and Private Sectors
    • Building strong alliances with government agencies and private organizations to pool resources, expertise, and support for shelter operations.
  2. Enhancing Quality of Care
    • Focusing on improving the standards of care in shelters, including medical services, nutritional support, and overall living conditions to ensure dignity and comfort for residents.
  3. Fostering Stakeholder Collaboration
    • Actively engaging with a wide network of stakeholders, including healthcare providers, NGOs, and community leaders, to create a comprehensive support system for the destitute.

Inclusion & Empowerment

Empowering voices, Changing Lives

Dream India Network’s Transgender Empowerment Programs are designed to foster inclusivity, equality, and empowerment for transgender individuals. By addressing the social, economic, and emotional challenges faced by the community, we strive to champion their right to a dignified life and equal opportunities.

Impact at a Glance

  • Supported nearly 1,500 transgender individuals with essential assistance during the pandemic, ensuring they had access to critical resources in a time of need.
  • Empowered around 600 individuals with vocational training across diverse skill sets, paving the way for economic independence and self-sufficiency.

Program Highlights

  • Comprehensive Assistance: During COVID-19, provided food, nutrition, and essential support to nearly 1,500 transgender individuals.
  • Skill Development: Facilitated skill training in tailoring, cooking, and computer literacy, enhancing livelihood opportunities.
  • Educational Support: The Transform Project aids hundreds in their pursuit of education, underscoring our dedication to transgender rights and well-being.
  • Medical Assistance: Providing regular health check-ups and medical interventions.
  • Community Engagement: Hosting cultural events to build bridges between transgender individuals and wider society.

This program exemplifies Dream India Network’s unwavering dedication to creating a society where transgender individuals are not only recognized but celebrated for their unique contributions. By addressing systemic barriers and fostering inclusivity, we aim to ensure that every transgender person has the opportunity to live a fulfilling and purposeful life.

Skill Development for Women & Youth

Empowering Women & Youth to Shape a Better Tomorrow.

At Dream India Network (DIN), we are committed to the belief that skill development is more than just learning—it’s a pathway to transformation. Women and youth hold the key to building stronger, more strong communities, and we are here to empower them with tools that pave the way for self-sufficiency and economic independence.

Through carefully designed training programs, we help individuals unlock their true potential, enabling them to contribute meaningfully to their families and society. By fostering skills that align with the needs of today’s workforce, we are cultivating a cycle of empowerment that uplifts not just individuals but entire communities.

Skill development is not just about providing knowledge, it is about creating opportunities. When individuals are equipped with practical, employable skills, they gain the confidence to pursue careers, start businesses, and lead meaningful lives. At DIN, we focus on programs empowering individuals to become financially independent. Each course is crafted to meet the demands of the modern job market. By nurturing leaders and entrepreneurs, we are fostering positive, long-lasting change.

Our Vocational Training Program Highlights

Dream India Network operates 20 training centers across India, each serving as a hub for learning and empowerment. Through these centers, we have trained nearly 450 individuals, providing them with employable skills that align with the demands of their local communities. These training centers act as lifelines for individuals seeking meaningful opportunities and a stepping stone toward a brighter future.

  • From tailoring and design to IT and technology skills, our programs are tailored to ensure immediate applicability in real-world settings.
  • Our courses are designed to meet the needs of participants from all walks of life, helping them take their first steps toward a rewarding career.
  • By focusing on in-demand skills, we ensure that our participants are prepared to thrive in today’s workforce.
  • Many have started ventures in fields like tailoring, handicrafts, and IT services, becoming self-employed entrepreneurs and contributing to their local economies.
  • These individuals not only achieve personal success but also serve as role models, showing others what’s possible through hard work and determination.
  • By earning a sustainable income, they support their families and invest in their communities, creating a ripple effect of positive change.

At Dream India Network, we see every individual as a seed of potential. Through skill development, we nurture that potential and help it grow into something extraordinary. With our focus on training for women and youth, we are building a brighter future, one where everyone has the tools to succeed and the confidence to lead.

Join us in unlocking potential and transforming lives. Together, we can create a future where empowerment is not just a goal but a way of life.

Medical Assistance

Healing without Boundaries

The Medical Support Program embodies our unwavering commitment to ensuring equitable healthcare for all. Recognizing that financial constraints and poverty often deny individuals access to essential medical care, this initiative is designed to extend a helping hand to those most in need. The program focuses on providing medical assistance to underprivileged individuals across India, addressing the critical healthcare challenges faced by marginalized communities. We strive to break down barriers to accessible healthcare, offering solutions that are both compassionate and impactful. By addressing urgent healthcare needs, the program ensures that no individual is left to suffer due to lack of resources.

Program Highlights

  • Identifying Needs: Proactively identifying individuals and communities most in need of medical support, especially those impacted by poverty.
  • Bridging Healthcare Gaps: Facilitating access to essential healthcare services, including diagnostics, treatment, and preventive care.
  • Overcoming Financial Barriers: Ensuring that financial constraints do not prevent the underprivileged from receiving quality medical care.
  • Partnerships for Health: Collaborating with healthcare providers, government programs, and other NGOs to maximize the reach and efficacy of our services.
  • Health Education and Awareness: Conducting awareness campaigns to educate communities on health, hygiene, and preventive measures to avoid common health issues.

Dream India Network envisions a future where healthcare is universally recognized as a fundamental right, accessible to everyone regardless of their financial circumstances. Our Medical Support Services are a testament to this belief, focusing on bridging the gap between the underserved and essential healthcare services.

Research, Training and Advocacy

Driving Social Change through Knowledge and Action

At Dream India Network (DIN), we understand that real, lasting social change stems from a deep understanding of the challenges faced by marginalized communities. By integrating research, training, and advocacy, we ensure that our interventions are not only well-informed but also impactful, actionable, and sustainable. Our approach isn’t just about addressing surface-level issues, it’s about transforming the systemic barriers that hinder progress, creating lasting solutions that empower underserved populations.

We believe that true change requires more than just good intentions, it requires data-driven insights, skillful action, and strategic influence. Through our work, we are causing transformations that reach far beyond the immediate community, reshaping policies, practices, and perceptions that affect the lives of millions.

At the core of Dream India Network’s initiatives is a commitment to rigorous, empirical research. We understand that to create effective, sustainable change, we must first understand the problems we are trying to solve. Our research efforts focus on:

  • We explore key issues affecting marginalized groups, such as poverty, gender inequality, education access, healthcare, and unemployment.
  • By analyzing community needs and examining existing systems, we identify patterns, barriers, and opportunities that inform our strategies.
  • With our research, we craft interventions that are relevant, context-specific, and proven to make a real difference.
